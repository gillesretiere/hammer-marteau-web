Privacy

CC Sudoku, for iPhone and iPad

In short: CC Sudoku collects no data. No account, no advertising, no analytics, no connection to the Internet. Everything the app remembers stays on your device.

What the app keeps, and where

To work, CC Sudoku stores on your iPhone or iPad, and nowhere else:

  • your games in progress, so you can resume them;
  • your settings: theme, gesture, notes, Zen mode;
  • your statistics: completed grids, records, daily streak.

This information never leaves your device. We have no access to it. It is deleted if you delete the app.

What the app does not do

  • It asks for no name, no email address and no account.
  • It does not use your location, camera, microphone or contacts.
  • It shows no advertising and contains no tracking or usage-analytics tools.
  • It does not connect to the Internet.

Apple

Downloading the app and, if you have agreed to it, crash reports go through Apple. They are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Since it collects no data, it collects none about children either.

Changes

If a future version of the app changed any of these rules, this page would be updated before its release, and the date below would change.
