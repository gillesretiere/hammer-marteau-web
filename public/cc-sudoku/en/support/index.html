Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
The guide opens at your first game. You can find it at any time in the menu, under « Comment jouer ».
How does the hint work?
It puts you on the right track in three steps. Expliquer (Explain) gives the technique and the area to look at. Voir (Show) displays the pattern in colour with the full reasoning. Appliquer (Apply) plays the move. Explain and Show each cost 5 points; reopening the same hint costs nothing more.
How do I show the candidates?
Menu › « Afficher les notes ». To remove a candidate, use the « Biffer » (strike out) tool below the grid.
Is my game saved?
Yes, after every move. Resume it from the menu, under « Charger une partie ». Games are stored on your device only: they are deleted if you delete the app.
I can't hear the music.
CC Sudoku stays silent when your iPhone is in silent mode, and gives way to your own music or podcasts if another sound is already playing. In Zen mode, the music is off.
What is Zen mode?
A game without points or music, to play quietly. Turn it on in the menu; it applies from the next game.
How are points and stars counted?
A deducible digit scores 10 points; a correct but guessed digit, 5. Each completed row, column or box adds a bonus. A grid finished with no hint and no mistake earns three stars.
