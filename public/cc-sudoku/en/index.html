CC Sudoku

For iPhone and iPad

A precise sudoku in a calm atmosphere, punctuated by a cuckoo when you unlock a digit. When you're stuck, it explains the technique before giving you the answer.

Coming soon to the App Store

The app is in French for now.

A hint in three steps

  • Explain: the name of the technique, how it works and where to look. Often that's enough to find the move yourself.
  • Show: the cells that form the pattern, in colour, with the full reasoning.
  • Apply: the move is played, if you want it.

Six levels, from Beginner to Diabolical

Each grid is rated by the techniques it really requires: singles at Beginner, then pairs, X-Wing and Y-Wing, up to the chains of the Diabolical level. Every grid has a unique solution and can be solved by logic alone, without guessing.

Reasoning pays

A deduced digit scores 10 points, a lucky guess only 5. Finish a grid with no hint and no mistake to earn three stars. Your statistics track your grids, your records and your daily streak.

A game you can hear

The music follows your game: quiet at first, it grows richer as the grid fills up. Zen mode turns off music and scoring, for a quiet game.

Nothing leaves your device

No ads, no account, no data collection. The app never connects to the Internet. Privacy policy
